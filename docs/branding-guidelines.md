## Non-Negotiables

- Keep the white + navy + gold identity.
- Keep trust-first, practical, non-hype messaging.
- Keep typography pairing: Fraunces for headings, Manrope for body/UI.
- Keep realistic business imagery.
- Keep member/admin portal shell consistency.
