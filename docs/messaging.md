# TASA Trust Messaging Guidelines

Last updated: 2026-02-07

## 1) Brand Foundation

### Brand name
- TASA Trust Pte. Ltd.
- Acronym: Team of Accountability, Skilled and Agility.

### Brand promise
- Professional Singapore corporate services delivered with trust, speed, and operational clarity.

### Positioning statement
- TASA Trust helps startups, SMEs, and overseas founders establish and run compliant businesses in Singapore through virtual office, accounting, tax, and corporate support.

### Audience
- Founders launching in Singapore.
- SMEs needing reliable compliance and back-office services.
- Remote operators who need local credibility through local address and support.

## 2) Messaging Architecture

### Core pillars
- Credibility: premium Singapore business presence.
- Reliability: accountable, compliant, professional delivery.
- Practical speed: fast setup, low friction, no unnecessary complexity.
- All-in-one support: virtual office + accounting + tax + corp-sec.

### Homepage core messages
- "Singapore's Trusted Virtual Office Provider"
- "Your Professional Business Address in Singapore"
- "Instant setup", "No lock-in contracts", "Cancel anytime"

### Proof language
- Use specific, operationally real claims.
- Prefer concrete outcomes over abstract adjectives.
- Example: "Get started in as little as 24 hours."

## 3) Tone of Voice

### Voice traits
- Professional
- Trustworthy
- Direct
- Helpful
- Non-hype

### Writing rules
- Use plain business English.
- Keep sentences short and outcome-driven.
- Avoid hype, slang, and inflated claims.
- Explain compliance and service scope clearly.

### Do / Don't
- Do: "Use for ACRA company registration."
- Do: "We'll respond within 24 hours."
- Don't: "Revolutionary growth engine."
- Don't: "Guaranteed instant success."

## 4) CTA Language

### Primary CTA style
- Action-first and specific.
- Examples:
  - "Get Your Address Today"
  - "Get Started Now"
  - "View Plans & Pricing"

### Secondary CTA style
- Consultation or low-pressure next step.
- Examples:
  - "Book a Consultation"
  - "Schedule a Call"
  - "Explore All Services"

## 5) Content Governance

### Copy review checklist
- Is the claim specific and credible?
- Is the service benefit clear in one sentence?
- Is there a clear next action?
- Does the tone remain professional and calm?
